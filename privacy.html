DOWNI — Privacy

DOWNI is built to need as little of your data as possible. Short version: no accounts, no tracking, and download history stays on your device.

The Android app

  • Downloads happen entirely on your device.
  • Your Vault, settings and download history never leave the phone.
  • The app talks only to the media platforms you paste links from.

The web app

  • Links you paste are processed by our serverless API to resolve the media — nothing is stored afterwards.
  • Your download history lives in your browser's local storage, not on our servers.
  • Standard hosting (Vercel) applies for serving the site itself.
